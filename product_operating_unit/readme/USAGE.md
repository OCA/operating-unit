1. Assign operating units to a product category. A product inherits the
   units of its category when created or when its category changes.
2. When you add units to a category, they are added to products in that
   category and its descendants. Removing units from a category leaves
   previously assigned product units in place.
3. For reading, users can see products and categories with at least one
   of their allowed units, as well as records without units, subject to
   the standard Odoo access rules.
